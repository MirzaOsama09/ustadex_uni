Who is Ustadex Insights built for? expand_more

Ustadex Insights is designed specifically for university leadership (Vice Chancellors, Provosts), Quality Enhancement Cells (QECs), ORIC directors, and accreditation teams who need a reliable, single source of truth to manage institutional performance and statutory compliance.

How does Ustadex help with university rankings? expand_more

Our platform automatically maps your raw institutional data directly to the rigorous criteria required by global ranking bodies like QS and THE (Times Higher Education). It tracks your metrics on a unified dashboard so you can spot performance gaps and prioritize strategic interventions early.

Can it integrate with our existing campus systems? expand_more

Yes. Ustadex acts as an ingestion hub that unifies legacy ERP, LMS, and HR systems. Instead of manually consolidating fragmented spreadsheets across different faculties, our automated connectors pool everything into one verified, secure source of truth.

How does the "Evidence-Backed" reporting work? expand_more

Ustadex uses over 500+ configurable data points to codify operational standards. When you generate a compliance report (like for HEC or PEC) or query the system, every claim is linked directly to cited institutional evidence, ensuring your audits are completely accurate and free of subjective bias.
